# Provenance for `rocket_episodes_seed.csv`

This seed table is **coarse, episode-level, and approximate**. It exists so the
rocket baseline can be explored before the ACLED pipeline is wired up. Treat
every number as an order-of-magnitude figure, not a precise count, and prefer
the ACLED-derived dataset for any published claim.

## Known limitations
- "Fired / intercepted / fell short" counts originate substantially from the
  **IDF and Israeli reporting** — i.e. one party to the conflict. Cross-check
  against ACLED and UN OCHA before relying on them.
- Episodes overlap with quieter periods not captured here; cumulative rows
  (1, 5) blend many small events and are the least reliable.
- "Success/failure" of a rocket is **not** published per-rocket anywhere. The
  closest proxies are *fell-short-in-Gaza* (failed to reach Israel) and
  *intercepted* (reached Israeli airspace but neutralized). Both are aggregates.
